# Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

## 1. Data Deletion

Users may submit a data deletion request at any time by contacting support@example.com or through the account settings page. The company will process all deletion requests within 30 calendar days of receipt. Upon completion, a confirmation email will be sent to the registered address. Note that certain records may be retained as required by applicable law even after a deletion request is fulfilled.

## 2. Third-Party Sharing

User data will not be shared with third parties without explicit written consent, except as required by law. In cases where data sharing is legally mandated, the company will notify the user unless prohibited from doing so by the relevant legal authority. Aggregated, anonymized data that cannot identify individual users may be shared with analytics partners without prior consent.

## 3. Cookies and Tracking

This platform uses essential cookies for authentication and optional analytics cookies. Essential cookies are required for the platform to function and cannot be disabled. Analytics cookies collect anonymized usage data to help improve the service. Users may disable analytics cookies at any time through the Cookie Preferences panel in account settings or via their browser settings.

## 4. Age Requirements

Users must be at least 18 years old to create an account. Minors between 13 and 17 may use the service only with verified parental consent. A parent or legal guardian must complete the consent form available on the registration page. Users who are found to be under 13 years of age will have their accounts suspended immediately and their data deleted within 7 days.

## 5. Marketing Communications

Users may opt out of marketing emails at any time via the unsubscribe link included in every communication, or through the notifications section of account settings. Opt-out requests are processed within 5 business days. Note that transactional emails such as receipts, security alerts, and account notifications are not classified as marketing and cannot be disabled.

## 6. Data Retention

Personal data is retained for the duration of the account's active status plus an additional 12 months after account closure, unless a deletion request is submitted. Financial transaction records are retained for 7 years to comply with tax and audit regulations. Log data is retained for a maximum of 90 days before automatic deletion.

## 7. Security

The company employs industry-standard encryption (AES-256) for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. In the event of a data breach affecting user personal information, affected users will be notified within 72 hours of the company becoming aware of the breach, in accordance with applicable data protection regulations.
